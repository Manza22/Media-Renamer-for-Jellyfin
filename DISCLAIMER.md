# Disclaimer & acceptable use

## Intended use

Media Renamer is **open-source software** (MIT License) for **renaming and organizing video files** in a personal or household media library, typically before or after importing into [Jellyfin](https://jellyfin.org/) or similar servers you control.

It is **not** security software, backup software, or a download tool for copyrighted content.

## Acceptable use

You may use, modify, and share this project under the [MIT License](LICENSE), including:

- Running it on your own machines
- Forking and improving it
- Publishing your own builds **if** you are honest about what they do and do not harm users

## Unacceptable use

The authors **do not permit or support** using this codebase (or confusingly similar branded builds) to:

- Distribute **malware**, **spyware**, **ransomware**, or **trojans**
- Hide malicious behavior inside a fake “media renamer” installer
- Trick users into running code they do not understand
- Exfiltrate API keys, media paths, or personal data to third parties without clear disclosure

If you fork this project, **do not** imply your modified build is the official release unless the maintainers agree.
